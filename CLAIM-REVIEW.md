## Source errors corrected

- Pergola FAQ contained a gas-fire-pit answer. Replaced with a relevant planning question and answer grounded in the pergola service description.
- Duplicated “Is it cheaper to build or buy a pergola?” FAQ consolidated; substantive comparison retained.
- Pergola page “Why choose us” heading referred to outdoor fire installation. Corrected.
- Patio service-area FAQ repeated an unrelated custom-design answer. Corrected to the published coverage.
- Removed an unverified retailer comparison and a categorical property-value claim from pergola FAQs; preserved the underlying custom-design and outdoor-use topics.
- Softened categorical “without cracking or shifting” and “no surprise costs/no delays” language. These are not defensible universal performance guarantees.
- Removed date-sensitive “over 15 years” claims from service details. Founding year remains the evidence-backed reference.

The exact before/after changes are in content-corrections.json. source-claim-inventory.csv catalogs original business claims. Original source snapshots remain intact.

## Limits

Licensing, insurance, local permit statements, source cost ranges and source project-duration estimates remain source statements in the detailed content, not independently certified facts. They are not promoted as verified badges, guaranteed prices or deadlines. The underlying 90 editorial blog articles were preserved, not subjected to an independent technical fact-check. Current contract terms, insurance and licensing evidence require the client's own records.

This SvelteKit export defaults to noindex for review. The export does not change the client domain or the published redesign's access settings. Forms still require delivery activation/verification before live traffic, and visual browser QA was not available in this environment.
